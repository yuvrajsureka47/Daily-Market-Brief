The Iran diplomacy trade is not a resolution — it is a reprieve. Markets have priced in a deal that has not been signed. Iran's Supreme Leader's advisor warned against American vessels in the same news cycle as Trump's diplomatic olive branch. Oil's 5% drop and the equity rally are both built on expectation, not fact. A single escalation incident reverses this trade within hours.

Manufacturing strength and Fed rate hike risk are pulling in opposite directions on equities. ISM at 55.6 with employment back in expansion is textbook good economic news. But with the 10-year yield at 4.688% and futures markets still pricing in a 33-55% chance of additional rate hikes, strong data is also a rate risk. The bond market is not celebrating the ISM print the way equities are — that divergence is worth watching.
03

It combines first-ever public financials, a $116 billion lock-up expiration on August 6, and the first disclosed look at AI infrastructure capex inside a company trading at 80x revenue. A miss on Starlink margin or AI monetization guidance could break confidence in the largest IPO in history.

India's FII posture tells a more cautious story than the headline index move suggests. FIIs bought Rs 922 crore in cash on August 3 but simultaneously held a net short index-futures position of 1,50,816 contracts. That is a hedged book — long on individual stocks, short on the index. It means foreign institutions are not running unprotected India exposure even as they participate in the rally. DII support is doing more structural work than FII conviction.
