China Manufacturing PMI: Official at 49.8 but Private Survey Surprises at 51.5
A split picture emerges. The official NBS Manufacturing PMI for August came in at 49.8, a second consecutive month of contraction, as weak domestic demand and typhoon disruptions weighed on activity. The divergence reflects that larger state enterprises remain sluggish while agile export-oriented firms are picking up AI infrastructure and tech-related orders. EU AI Act Disclosure Requirements Now Live; Agent Deployments Accelerate Globally
