CHIP DESIGN
Google's "Frozen v2" Chip Could Embed Gemini Directly Into Hardware
The Information reported that Google is developing a new chip codenamed Frozen v2 that would include bits of Gemini (Google's AI model) embedded directly into the chip architecture, reducing inference latency and processing time significantly. This moves the AI stack closer to the hardware layer, reducing reliance on cloud round-trips for model execution. Strategic importance: if successful, this signals a shift toward edge AI inference (running AI models directly on devices rather than sending data to cloud servers), which competes with Nvidia's software ecosystem and opens a new front in the AI hardware race.
