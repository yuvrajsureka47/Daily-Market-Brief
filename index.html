ECB Decision Thursday — Hold Expected at 2.25%, But Oil Makes it Uncertain

Prediction markets show a 98.7% probability of no change. EU Fines AliExpress €550M Under Digital Services Act
The European Commission levied a €550 million (approximately $629 million) fine on AliExpress for failing to prevent the sale of illegal and counterfeit goods to EU consumers. This is the most significant enforcement action under the Digital Services Act to date. It signals that EU tech regulation is now entering a punitive execution phase — creating compliance cost risk for any large platform operating in European markets.

AI Watch
Infrastructure Deal
Meta and Anthropic in Early Talks for $10 Billion Compute Lease
Anthropic proposed renting AI data center capacity from Meta in a deal worth up to $10 billion over two years, confirmed by Reuters, CNBC, and the New York Times. Payments would run in monthly installments with an early-exit clause. This follows Anthropic's $45 billion, three-year compute deal with SpaceX signed in May. Anthropic has placed usage limits on its most advanced models due to compute shortages even at near-$1 trillion valuation, with a planned IPO reportedly targeted for October 2026. For Meta, this is the first concrete step toward monetizing its $145 billion 2026 capital expenditure — creating a cloud business to rival CoreWeave and Nebius. The unusual dynamic: Meta and Anthropic are AI competitors, yet compute scarcity has made such cross-competitor infrastructure deals routine industry practice.

A policy mistake in either direction has large consequences. Watch the press conference language at 14:30 CET Thursday as carefully as the rate decision itself.
