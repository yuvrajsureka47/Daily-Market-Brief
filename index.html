Policy

US tightens AI chip access for data centers serving China

Fresh · Mon 16 Nov, Commerce Department interim rule
The rule requires licenses to export advanced AI chips to Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia and Brazil, and overseas data centers supplying significant computing power to firms headquartered in China must obtain licenses within 90 days. US headquartered operators are carved out, and the rule takes effect once published in the Federal Register. Nvidia declined to comment. Why it matters: it closes the route Chinese AI firms used to rent advanced chips abroad, and it helped drive the Asian chip crash.
