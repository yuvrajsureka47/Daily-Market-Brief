Regulation
Anthropic's Fable 5 Access Now Requires Usage Credits and ID Verification
Starting July 8, Anthropic's Fable 5 model requires usage credits for all subscriber tiers, priced at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, billed on top of subscriptions. Additionally, government-issued ID verification via Persona takes effect today, gating access for unverified users. Claude Sonnet 5 (introductory pricing at $2/$10 through August 31) and Opus 4.8 ($5/$25) remain on standard subscription plans. This is the first major frontier model access restriction with a hard compliance date, and it sets a precedent for how AI labs manage safety obligations while maintaining commercial growth.
