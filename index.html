Wall Street caps its best week since April 2025 with a broad half day rally

The S&P 500 rose 1.60% to 7,297.49 (up 5.6% on the week), the Nasdaq Composite rose 2.31% to 24,129.50 (up 6.68%), the Nasdaq 100 rose 2.61% to 26,726.39, and the Dow rose 591.99 points (1.18%) to 50,774.94 (up 5.46%). Nvidia closed up only 0.80% at $150.49 after trading weak for most of the day, held back by a Bloomberg report about advanced Nvidia hardware in China. Why it matters: volume was thin and the session was only 3.5 hours, and November is still down: the S&P 500 by 3.7%, the Nasdaq by 5.1% and the Dow by 1.9%, with the S&P 500 still 6.7% below its October 6 high.

Fresh · Fri 27 Nov, bond market close about 2:00 PM ET

Yields rise as money moves from bonds into stocks, and a December Fed hike stays priced

The 10 year Treasury yield rose 9 bp (basis points, 0.01% each) to 4.94%, as stock gains reduced demand for safe haven bonds, and the dollar index rose 0.33% to 104.01 (one source). Fed funds futures price about a 99% chance of a 25 bp hike at the December 8 to 9 meeting (decision December 9), bets on a half point move have faded, and November CPI arrives the day after the decision. Gold rebounded about 3% and silver about 9%. Why it matters: with the hike priced, the market’s question is whether services inflation starts to cool, and a long rally in stocks is pushing yields up, not down, which was the pattern all week.

Fresh · Tue 24 to Fri 27 Nov

Oil ends a three week losing streak ahead of Sunday’s OPEC+ meeting

Brent settled Friday down $1.13 (1.2%) at $95.44, after touching its highest since November 9 intraday, and gained about 3% on the week, its first weekly rise in November after three weekly losses. WTI settled at $83.08, down 60 cents (0.7%) from Wednesday (there was no Thursday settlement), and its weekly change is reported as down 0.4% to 0.9%, depending on the source. Attacks kept supply fears alive: a Houthi drone struck a tower in Riyadh’s financial district on November 24 (at least 11 injured, per Al Jazeera), Saudi Arabia said Houthis disrupted operations at its Jazan refinery, and Kuwait said Iranian drones hit a vital facility and killed two Italian technicians, which Iran denies. OPEC+ ministers meet Sunday, and three sources told Reuters the group is set to hold first quarter 2027 output targets unchanged, while Iraq presses for a higher production ceiling. Why it matters: Brent’s January contract expires Monday, so the first trading after the meeting is also an expiry day.

Why it matters: foreign money kept buying through Friday’s fall, so the sell off came from profit taking, not from foreigners leaving. Provisional figures can be revised.
