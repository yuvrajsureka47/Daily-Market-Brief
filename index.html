AI Watch
Chips and Policy

Nvidia pauses server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries, and S&P flags a possible downgrade

Fresh · Thu 26 Nov reports
The Financial Times reported, citing three people, that Nvidia told manufacturing partners such as Dell and Supermicro not to ship servers containing its chips to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia. It is unclear whether the halt is temporary. It follows a Commerce Department interim rule that took effect November 19, requiring licenses for advanced AI chip exports to data centers in those countries and Brazil, and the Entity List addition of Singapore’s Megaspeed International and affiliates. S&P Global Ratings also put Nvidia’s AA rating on CreditWatch Negative, citing governance and “key man” risks, while Moody’s said its Aa1 rating stands. Nvidia closed Wednesday at $149.30, about 10% lower over two days after Monday’s 31.48% jump. Why it matters: Southeast Asia is a large source of Nvidia revenue, so a prolonged pause hits sales, and the credit action adds a new governance question.
