Fed minutes: almost all officials expect more rate hikes

Minutes of the October 27 to 28 meeting said almost all participants judged further hikes would likely be appropriate. Anthropic prices a record IPO at $83 and debuts today as ANT

Fresh · Priced Wed 18 Nov evening ET; first trade Thu 19 Nov
Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share, above the $70 to $78 range, and upsized it to 980 million Class A shares, raising about $81.3 billion, more than SpaceX’s $75 billion base deal (CNBC). CNBC puts the fully diluted market value near $1.5 trillion. Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC separately agreed to buy $26 billion of non voting shares at the IPO price, underwriters hold an option for more shares, and the offering is expected to close on November 20. Trading on Nasdaq under ANT is due Thursday with no scheduled time for the first trade, set by Nasdaq’s opening auction. Bloomberg reported orders about six times the shares on offer, and Amazon (+3.8%) and Alphabet (+0.8%) rose Wednesday on their stakes. Why it matters: it is the biggest IPO on record and a direct test of appetite for AI. No first trade price was available at the newest timestamp.

Earnings

Nvidia beats and raises, but the stock still falls on leadership and margins

Context · Results Tue 17 Nov after the close; stock reaction Wed 18 Nov
Nvidia reported fiscal Q3 revenue of $115.84 billion, up 103%, against $109.1 billion expected (LSEG), with data center revenue of $107.6 billion and adjusted EPS of $2.68 against $2.47 expected. It guided Q4 revenue to $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, against about $125 billion expected, including an extra week and assuming no China data center compute revenue, and raised its fiscal 2028 growth outlook to 75% from 70%. Gross margin is guided down to 71% to 72% from 74.8%. The stock rose before the open Wednesday, then closed down 2.89% at $150.96, its lowest close since June 2025, after board chair Harvey Jones said on the call that Jensen Huang will not return as CEO. Why it matters: the numbers were strong, but the market is pricing leadership, margin and China risk. A new Commerce Department chip export rule from Monday is a further overhang.
