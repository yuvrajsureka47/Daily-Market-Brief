Iran's position has hardened, a tanker was attacked over the weekend, and Houthi militants struck Saudi Arabia's Jazan refinery. The war premium in oil has returned at roughly $10-15/bbl above a normal supply/demand price. This feeds directly into US, European, and Indian inflation, complicates every central bank's calculus, and creates a second-order risk that higher energy prices force the Fed, ECB, and RBI into positions they do not want to take.

By partnering with six of the world's largest asset managers to create "compute financing platforms," Nvidia is transforming AI chips from products into infrastructure-class investable assets. This draws insurance capital, pension money, and long-duration institutional capital into AI capex. It also solves the AI financing bottleneck that was beginning to form. Goldman Sachs estimates global AI infrastructure investment will exceed $1 trillion in 2026. The circular nature of the financing is a risk worth monitoring.
04

The reason is structural: in a genuine geopolitical crisis environment with oil supply risk, central bank rate uncertainty, and dollar weakness, institutions and sovereigns turn to gold. Bitcoin still behaves as a risk asset in crisis conditions, not a store of value in the way gold behaves. Watch whether this changes post-CPI print.

Analysts expect targeted measures rather than broad easing, which means the China-driven commodity reflation trade that many EM investors have positioned for may not materialize in the near term. Watch the yuan and Shanghai Composite as leading signals.
