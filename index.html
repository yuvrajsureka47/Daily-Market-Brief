JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon Flags Hidden Leverage Risk
In a CNBC interview, Dimon warned that margin debt is at all-time highs, and much of it is not visible because it sits in hedge fund prime brokerage accounts, ETF structures, and Treasury arbitrage positions. Hidden leverage amplifies selloffs. When a large player gets a margin call (a demand to deposit more money to keep a position open), it triggers forced selling that cascades through markets. Dimon's warning is not a near-term trade call, but it signals that the system is fragile at elevated valuations, particularly if a hot NFP or inflation print forces the Fed's hand in September.
