Nvidia reinstates Huang after a week of scandal and a stock crash

Fresh · Sun 22 Nov decision, Mon 23 Nov announcement
Nvidia’s board voted unanimously on Sunday to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director, effective immediately, saying it received input from senior levels of the US government. President Trump said he spoke with the board “directly and indirectly” and told CNBC Huang “did nothing wrong.” The independent committee’s investigation will continue, and a new National Security and Export Control Committee has been formed. The stock rose 31.48% to $165.05, with upgrades from Melius, Bernstein, StoneX and Oppenheimer. Context: Nvidia beat on revenue and guidance on November 17 (fourth quarter guide of $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, with no China data center compute assumed) yet fell 22.92% on the week, including 14.01% to $125.53 on Friday after Reuters reported SEC enforcement staff are looking at possible accounting issues, which Reuters said does not establish wrongdoing. Why it matters: the rally shows how much the AI trade leans on one company, and the investigations and White House involvement are still open risks.
