PBOC acts to slow the yuan's rally

Democratic sweep puts the data center build out on the political agenda

Democrats won the Texas governor race after campaigning for tougher data center limits, plus key races in Ohio, Wisconsin and Georgia. Voters rejected data centers in all but one of 41 ballot measures across 10 states. Hut 8 fell 23%, IREN 11% and Core Scientific 10%, while Microsoft rose 6.7% and Amazon 6.1%.
