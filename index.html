Anthropic’s record IPO opens 34% higher and closes 17% above its price

Fresh · Thu 19 Nov, first day of trading
Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share on November 18, above the $70 to $78 range, and sold 980 million shares (up from 825 million), raising about $81.3 billion. Shares began trading on the Nasdaq as ANT at about 11:48 AM ET, opened at $111.50, traded between $92.72 and $115.10, and closed at $97.17, up 17.07%. Earnings

Nvidia beat and raised, but the stock has fallen six sessions in a row

Context · Tue 17 Nov results, with Thu 19 close
Nvidia reported fiscal Q3 revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago and above the roughly $109 billion expected, with data center revenue of $107.6 billion and non GAAP earnings per share of $2.68 against $2.47 expected. Its fourth quarter guide is $130 billion, plus or minus 3%, and it assumes no data center compute revenue from China. The board also raised its buyback authorisation by $150 billion, and Chairman Harvey Jones said the board has no intention of reinstating Jensen Huang. The stock closed Thursday at $145.98, down 3.30%, after erasing an early 4.7% gain. Why it matters: a large beat and a higher guide failed to stop the selling, so the AI trade is now trading on rates, leverage and sentiment, not on earnings.
